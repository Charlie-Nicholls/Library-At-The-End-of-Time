###### Mirewood Church
<span class="sub2">:FasCircleQuestion: Church</span>

---

> [!boxed|no-t]
> The centerpiece of the town is a stone abbey, clearly the oldest building in the town aside from the tower. A large belltower stretches from the back of the building and a graveyard is to the side of the building. You can either go through the church door or explore the graveyard.
>^IntroText

### Inside

> [!boxed|no-t]
> The central room of the church is the main sanctuary, with a shrine to the gods located at the back, while wooden pews flank the sides of a central aisle. A few skeletons of priests and townsfolk are strewn about the room. A wooden door sits just behind the shrine, likely leading to the bell tower.
>^Inside

### Tower

> [!boxed|no-t]
> From this vantage point, the town of Mirewood spreads out before you, its maze of bridges and shacks nestled amongst the dense trees and flowing river. The menacing red glow of angry spirits appears and disappears among the structures, reminding you that you are not completely alone in this place. Hanging above you is an old bronze bell, its toll silent. A scroll with glowing green runes has been stabbed into the bell by an ornate dagger.
