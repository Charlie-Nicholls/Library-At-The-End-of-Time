### Description
The ground is made of parchment and the people and animals who live nearby subsist on the words that flow through, travelling from [[The Library]] to the [[The Realm of Fiction]] through the Sink/Source. The sky is also made of parchment and on a high perception the words can be seen to flow from the Source/Sink, through the horizon and back towards the Sink/Source in the centre of the village.

The people of the village are from the stories torn from [[The Library]] and used to make the spine of [[The Narrator's Prison]]. The proximity to the [[The Realm of Fiction]] caused some of them to be realised as people and objects. But as the [[Word Eater]]s ate their stories, they have since forgotten who they are or what their stories were.

### Source/Sink
At the centre of the village the ground falls away, leaving a hole in space through which the words travel. One side leads to [[The Library]]'s fiction section, from which words flow out of the source, through the rivers of words in the village and to the Sink. The sink leads to [[The Realm of Fiction]]. As space in the village is 4D, the sky periodically falls and becomes the floor, with the floor becoming the sky. Swapping whether the centre of the village is the Source or the Sink.
