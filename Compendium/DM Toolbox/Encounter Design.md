# 8 Types of Combat

**Skirmish**
Group of monsters without any clear advantage or disadvantage. Often in a confined space.

**Ambush**
Monsters get the jump on players. Can be used to make a normally simple encounter difficult. Enemies get to target weaker players first, move to strategic positions, bait players away from other hidden enemies.

**Targeted Strike**
Reverse of ambush, players get the jump on monsters. Can be used to make normally deadly encounters achievable. Give the players consumables before hand they can use here ahead of time and/or environmental features they can use with that chance to plan.
