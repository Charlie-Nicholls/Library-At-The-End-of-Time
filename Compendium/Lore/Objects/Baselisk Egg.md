### Description
Baselisks do not lay eggs the same way as other creatures. Instead, they seek out a rock that looks as much like an egg as possible. As creatures of myth, the rock becomes a baselisk egg if enough people are convinced that it is one. So the baselisk is compelled either to bring people to its egg or vice versa until enough people believe in it for it to hatch. As such, baselisks are able to reproduce without a partner or, in rare cases, arise spontaneously if people convince themselves they've found a baselisk egg without needing to see a baselisk. Though the belief they're purely mythical has made this very imporobable.
