### Text

> [!recite|clean no-t]
> 	I looked down at my latest warlock, I'm still not convinced of his competence. Tibword's report on his escapades in life was not exactly glowing, but skills I can teach him, morals are far harder to shape. It's always a risk to bring someone in, but the status quo can not be allowed to continue and we need to organise. "The Authors run The Library." I tell him, "They claim to work on behalf of the gods, Actus, Cognus and Mythos, but this seems increasingly doubtful. I lead an organisation called The Editors, we believe the great power granted to us by The Library should be used for good. The Authors have become too concerned with stability and non-interference to see the damage they could prevent." I can't help a sneer come into my voice as I say this, as an archetype so closely tied to the struggles of powerless, it infuriates me I can't openly help those who's ideas I represent.
> 	
> 	"We chose you because we believe you want to help also, but I need you to prove yourself before you can join properly. Tibword has exerted his influence to ensure your party will be allowed to conclude your adventure in The Badlands. We were forced to work with Eddie Van Patten to achieve our aims and obscure it as natural timeline fluctuation. But with your group being sent directly, we can be more precise with our influence. When you visit The Last Chance Saloon, a politician called John Wilde will be speaking there. In the normal timeline he is assassinated a few weeks later in a train derailment that kills many. Instead we got in early and contacted Eddie, offering him a way to make compact explosives to kill him at the speech. We don't know his exact plan, but this is the important thing: his son William will be there, and he must be killed. Save John, save as many bystanders as you can, so long as his son doesn't make it out alive. William Wilde later becomes known as Wilde Willie, a viscous mobster responsible for the death and suffering of thousands in The Badlands." Tibword had retrieved the replacement book they used to travel to The Badlands after we stole the original to delay them, and I handed it to him. "This is Wild-Life - The Mad Life of Wild Willie, a book which describes his life and all the atrocities he commits. Read it between now and your mission and you'll understand why he cannot be allowed to live."
> 	
> 	I smiled at him confidently, that of someone with complete belief that this will be a simple task. It won't of course, this is a massive risk I'm asking him to take, The Authors will likely send him back to his death if they discover him. But he doesn't need to know that, it will only make him nervous.
