### Description
The Archetypes are manifestations of character tropes in story. Ranking above [[The Librarians]] and below [[The Authors]] in the hierarchy in [[The Library]]. They have no defined physical forms, and can appear differently to different people depending on how they think about such characters. Often appearing as a personal favourite character that fits their archetype.
