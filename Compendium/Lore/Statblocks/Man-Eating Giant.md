###### Man-Eating Giant
<span class="sub2">:LiLayoutList: Construct </span>
___

> [!boxed|no-t]
> A huge creature in the likeness of a man, with eyes like coals of fire and eye-teeth like boar's tusks and a vast big gape like the mouth of a well. He has long loose lips like camel's, hanging down from his face, enormous ears falling over his shoulder-blades and the nails of his hands were like the claws of a lion.
