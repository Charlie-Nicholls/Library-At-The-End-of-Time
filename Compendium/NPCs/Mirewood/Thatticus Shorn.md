# Profile

> [!recite|clean no-t]
>	His clothing must have, at one point, been well made, something a nobleman would have worn, but it has now become soiled and tattered and, in some places, blood-stained. Over the top of his clothing, this man has constructed a sort of armour with what appear to be bones. In his hand he clutches close to him a book, kept in a relatively pristine condition compared to your surroundings. His hair is long and messy, brown streaked with white. However, his most outstanding feature is his face. Most of it appears normal, if a little gaunt; however, a large portion of his left side is rotted and decayed, as if he is himself partly a corpse.
