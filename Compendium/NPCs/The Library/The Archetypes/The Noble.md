### Description
The Noble is an [[The Archetypes|Archetype]] that represents nobility, establishment, the politically and socially powerful. They are the twin of [[The Trickster]], with The Noble claiming [[The Trickster]] is a liar, a con artist and a thief. While [[The Trickster]] would claim the same of The Noble. Some authors represent them as the heroes, champions of morality and law - particularly in older stories designed to promote the status quo. Whereas, in modern stores, they more often take on the role of villain - the established powers the protagonist fights against who lie, manipulate and profit their populace.

Many chapters ago, The Noble was the leader of [[The Authors]], but came undone when they began scheming to increase they power. They aimed to create a magical machine (?) that would generate endless books to expand [[The Library]]'s dominion and influence on mortals. More details.

But [[The Trickster]], who was initially working with The Noble had a crisis of conscience and decided to reveal his plan to the rest of [[The Authors]] before it could be completed. [[The Alternate Trickster]] is the version of [[The Trickster]] who decided not to do so. [[The Noble]] was expelled from [[The Library]] and is scheming to return to power.

#### The Museum Heist
On learning that [[The Trickster's Book]] was inside the [[Wandering Museum]], The Noble planned to take it to enact revenge. They took the form of a mortal, Dominic Bardi, using his powers to trick the families of [[Waterdeep]], including the Bardi family themselves into believing they had always been there. They donated the exhibits in the [[Wandering Museum#Area 17 Dominic Bardi Gallery]] to attempt to convince them to give them more control over their security, under the pretence of protecting their artifacts. They installed the [[Sphinx Statue]]s, programming them to ignore The Noble, but couldn't convince them to disable the *magic circle* enchantment that prevented them from entering. They then installed the incompetent [[Rinaldo Bardi]] as to guard to disable the *magic circle* and *forcecages* so he could come in and steal the book, but [[Rinaldo Bardi|Rinaldo]] was fired before they could enact their plan. Their latest gambit is to hire [[Florence the Cat]] to disable the two spells before he is due to visit on the final day it is in waterdeep, then create a distraction while they steal the book.
