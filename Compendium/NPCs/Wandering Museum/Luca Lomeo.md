# Profile

> [!boxed|no-t]
> Introduction for players
>^IntroText

### Description
Luca Lomeo is a graduate student starting the long climb of a career in the humanities. He is the museum's scribe, transcribing copies of any documents the curators need and keeping notes for the museum's meetings, transactions and events. He is very intelligent but needs to get more sleep.

Overworked, overqualified and underpaid, Luca has too much on his mind to be suspicious. **He rolls all Wisdom (Insight) checks with disadvantage. He assumes that anyone who gets into the staff areas has a reason to be there and distractedly answers any questions asked of him.** Luca knows how all of the runes function, where they are, and the password to deactivate the darkness spell in the archives: *lux et veritas*. If he becomes suspicious of the characters, he makes an excuse to leave and raises the alarm level of the museum by one.
