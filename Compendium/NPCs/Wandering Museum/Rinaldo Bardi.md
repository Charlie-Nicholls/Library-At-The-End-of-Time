# Profile

> [!recite|clean no-t]
>	A young man dressed in an overfine cloak and curled shoes, he keeps one eyebrow quirked in a fixed expression of ironic incredulity.
>^IntroText

### Description
Almost completely lacking in redeeming qualities, Rinaldo Bardi is arrogant, entitled, and foolish. He speaks mostly in sarcastic rhetorical questions. Rinaldo is the museum director's nephew, and he was recently a museum docent for less than two weeks. He is outraged at his dismissal and regularly comes to the museum to harass his former co-workers.

If the players ask Rinaldo about the museum security, he will happily tell them everything he knows. Learning that they want to steal something only increases his eagerness to help. He did not work at the museum for long and was terrible at his job, so his information is not detailed. 
- Forcecages: There is some kind of force field behind the exhibit glass.
- Runes: The runes on the walls have something to do with the force fields. There are a few in staff areas.
- Museum layout: Mostly offices, and a vault on the first floor where they keep stuff not on display. 

Rinaldo still has his staff key, and he will sell it to players for 300gp. It opens the doors in areas 3, 4, and 10.
