### Description
Travel to [[The Badlands]] and retrieve the book [[Practical Magichemsitry]] from [[Huck]] at [[Flintfall]].

[[Eddie Van Patten]] sent someone to hurt [[Huck]] a few weeks earlier, specifically to attempt to get him to loan out a magichemistry book to fix himself. He then returns a few hours before [[The Party]] arrive to retrieve the book, which also details instructions to create a magically enhanced explosive. This will then be set up at [[The Last Chance]] to attempt to kill [[John Wilde]], a politician running on a platform of cracking down on criminals like [[Eddie Van Patten]] who is proving resistant to bribes. The explosion would also kill [[Wild Willie|William Wilde]], [[John Wilde]]'s son and future criminal syndicate leader. [[The Authors]] mastermind this plan in an attempt to prevent the many crimes and suffering caused by [[Wild Willie]]. [[Chan-tho]] steals [[Abblesmith's Guide to the Badlands Desert]] before the players can use it to travel to [[Flintfall]], which they then use to travel there themselves to organise the plan and causing the delay that allows [[Eddie Van Patten]] to steal [[Practical Magichemsitry]].

# The Train Heist

> - Huck lays out 3 ways the train could be boarded
> 	- Obtaining disguises and boarding the train before it leaves [[Flintfall]]
> 	- Jumping onto the train from above when it passes under Venompoint Bridge and leaves [[Flintfall]]
> 	- Chasing after the train on horseback

> ### Flintfall Disguises
> **Pros:** Quiet and could avoid fighting | **Cons:** If discovered a large fight will break out
>> [!recite|clean no-t]
>>	As you make your way towards the station to wait for the train, you see a large locomotive, four cars long is parked their. The doors on the second car are open with two guards positioned either side, surveying the area.
> - [[Huck]] will have some Van Patten uniforms from previous arrest of his men
> - Requires a DC12 Dexterity (Stealth), Charisma (Deception), or Charisma (Performance) check to make it past the guards. Convincing lies give advantage.
> - There are two **Van Patten Mercenaries** guarding the entrance in their typical black and red outfits
> - If they fail, the party will have to fight their way on board or leave and try a different method. If they fight, two additional **Van Patten Mercenaries** come out from the second car to assist the others. The train also takes off after the third round.
> - If Successful, they are placed inside car two with two **Van Patten Mercenaries**. If they try to sneak out they will need to pass a DC15 Charisma (Deception) check to not tip off the guards as to why they're heading to the vault car.

> ### Venompoint Bridge Jump
> **Pros:** Quick, simple and more guaranteed than disguises | **Cons:** Guards will hear characters landing
>> [!recite|clean no-t]
>> 	A wooden trestle bridge spans over the dusty track, its timbers beginning to rumble as the train quickly approaches. You and your posse are stationed on the bridge above, prepared to make the jump as soon as the train passes underneath you."
> - Jumping on the train requires a DC10 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check.
> - If they fail, they land but take 2 bludgeoning damage. If they fail by 5 or more they slip and nearly fall off the train and must succeed a DC10 Strength or Dexterity saving throw to not fall off completely. Any character who has already landed can try to help if they themselves succeed a DC10 Strength or Dexterity saving throw.
> - If they fail again, they fall off the train and take 1d6 bludgeoning damage.
> - If they fail again, Huck arrives on a horse to pick them up and take them to the back of the train.
> - Characters with the ability to teleport magically and do so onto the train without needing to roll.
> - Once all characters have landed, the guards are alerted and two **Van Patten Mercenaries** climb up to stop them.
> - Inside the second car are two more **Van Patten Mercenaries**
> - The characters can bypass the guards by going through the storage car, but cannot unlock the vault until they get the key from the **Van Patten Conductor**

> ### Chase Down the Tracks
> **Pros:** Straightforward and has more time to deal with guards | **Cons:** Least stealthy and will involve an immediate fight
>> [!recite|clean no-t]
>> 	The line of cars snakes its way through the rocky desert landscape as the train rushes to get to its destination. You are in hot pursuit, slowly gaining on it as the horse upon which you ride gallops as fast as your spurs can encourage it to go.
> - Roll initiative and start 60 feet from the train. Their horses move them 20 feet closer at the start of each turn. When within 5 feet of the train they can easily climb a top the train or into the storage car.
> - There are three **Van Patten Mercenaries** on top of the train using crossbows to shoot at the characters with Disadvantage.
> - There is one **Van Patten Mercenary** in the guard car.

> ### The Train
> **1: Storage Car**
>> [!recite|clean no-t]
>>	This car is a simple storage car, filled with stacked boxes containing various types of equipment and supplies
> - This car is filled with boxes of weapons, equipment and uniforms. The characters can find shortswords, crossbows, bolts, leather armour and daggers.
> - If the characters made it hear stealthily, guards will not be alerted to their presence as the only door is to the vault and is locked.
> - They also find papers detailing the layout of [[The Last Chance]] Saloon with 'X's marked in a few places
>
>**2: The Locomotive**
>> [!recite|clean no-t]
>>	The locomotive sits at the head of the train, an engine of steam and magical instruments groaning and whining as the complex machine pulls the rest of the car at breakneck speed.
> - In here is the **Van Patten Conductor** who has the key to the vault on them
> - The characters can try to stealthily take it by rolling a Dexterity (Stealth) and Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) against the conductors passive perception score (10).
> - If they fail, the conductor attacks, even if they are disguised.
> - If they attempt to charm the conductor he will be suspicious as he knows everyone on the train unless they are magically disguised as one of the crew. He will ask their name and if they do not guess correctly (Billy) he will attack.
>
> **3: The Vault Car**
> - Made from an indestructible alloy the door to the vault is locked and requires a key to open.
> - Alternatively it can be opened with thieves tools on a successful DC 25 Dexterity check.
> - Inside the vault is over 50,000 gp, [[Practical Magichemsitry]], and a note that reads: "A book detailing the production of the necessary explosives is currently possessed by [[Huck]], the Sheriff of [[Flintfall]]. Then proceed to the Last Chance"
