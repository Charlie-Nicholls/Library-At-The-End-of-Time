### Description
The [[Tome of Spirits]] disappeared from [[The Library]] in chapters passed, but reports are that it might have surfaced in the town of [[Mirewood]]. [[The Party]] must travel to the town, and locate the book to bring it back to safety in [[The Library]]. However, [[Thatticus Shorn]] has found the book before them and begun to unleash his wrath on the town in his attempts to attain lichdom.

### Quest Giver
[[Tibword Phirin]] on behalf of [[The Librarians]] under instruction from [[The Trickster]]

> [!boxed|no-t]
> "Some chapters ago a necromantic book by the name of the Tome of Spirits vanished from The Library, it was found to be in the hands of a coven of witches in Mirewood, but the agents we sent there were unable to recover it. We thought it lost forever, but there are now reports, some hundreds of years later mortal time, that some evil has overtaken Mirewood. You're to go there and retrieve the Tome. You are authorized to use your best judgement in attempting to undo whatever evil the tome has caused and kill if necessary to stop further infection of the timeline."

### Objectives
 - [x] Retrieve the [[Tome of Spirits]]
 - [x] Free [[Mirewood]] From the curse
 - [x] Stop the necromantic effects

# Vortex of Souls

- [x] **Level up to level 3**

> [!boxed|no-t]
> As the ghosts of the Lothbrook family fade away; suddenly a strike of lightning explodes above you and the world around you spins and shifts. For a moment you see an ethereal duplication of the world, layered on top of the real one until it all combines together, forming a new plane around you. The trees become ghostly, gnarls in the wood become twisted faces, and branches become grasping hands for a moment before shifting back into their regular states. A storm of raging souls cascades overhead, originating from deeper into the swamps.

> [!recite|no-i nbrd] The Ferryman
>> [!boxed|no-t]
>> 	Paddling up the shoreline in a large wooden gondola you see a semi-spectral figure draped in tattered black robes that are adorned with golden edging and filigree. They wear a large black hat with a wide brim which messy hair dangles from underneath. You can only see the shadowy silhouette of their face, though their left eye pierces through that shadow, glowing a deep green.
> - [[The Ferryman]] stands upon a boat that they push along with a very long staff. They were a few rings, including a signet ring, the emblem of which is unfamiliar to the party. 
> - They offer the characters a ride to get closer to the vortex, though they can only risk going so far, they do not want to risk being pulled in themselves.
> - They provide the party with the following information:
>   - There are two paths to the epicenter of the vortex and each has its own unique dangers.
>   - The first path is the Deep Swamps, where vile creatures of the Realms of the Dead lurk. They note: *"All who fail to navigate her twisting roots and splintering vines fall victim to pain."*
>   - The second path is the Cave of Sorrow, where one might just lose themselves if they are not careful. They not: *"Your minds will be tested in the pitch black of the ethereal void."*
> - The party's steamboat has been crushed by a falling cyprus tree during the start of the vortex. 
> - If they refuse [[The Ferryman]]'s offer and go through the swamps on foot, it takes them a little over 3 hours to do. At the end of which, each character must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or else take one level of exhaustion.

> [!recite|no-i nbrd] Deep Swamps
>> [!boxed|no-t]
>> The shadows grow deeper and darker the further into the murky swamp as you go. The trees grow in height, towering above you, their gnarled branches reaching out like clawed hands, as if trying to grab you. The wind howls through the canopy, making the swamp itself moan in anguish.
> - The Ferryman takes them until they reach a denser point, ushering the characters off and wishing them good luck.
> - Describe how the trees have faces, and their branches and hands are trying to grab at them; how figures emerge from the murky water to try to drag them down into the mud; or how the wind itself sounds like whispering in their ears. Ask for a DC 15 skill check and have them describe what they do in order to traverse the swamps.
>
> **Total success.** The party makes it through the Deep Swamp unscathed and gains 3 temporary HP each.
> **Success.** While they manage to make their way out of the swamps, each character takes 1d6 slashing damage from the clawed branches to a minimum of 1 HP.
> **Failure.** The party still manages to traverse through the swamps, but each takes 2d6 slashing damage from the clawed branches to a minimum of 1 HP.
> **Total Failure.** Each character takes 1d6 slashing damage from the clawing branches and 2d6 psychic damage, as the endless swamps addled their minds and they witness horrors beyond comprehension to a minimum of 1 HP.

> [!recite|no-i nbrd] Cave of Sorrow
>> [!boxed|no-t]
>>  	Dug into the grassy hillock is a small opening, where large stones hold up the earth above it. Etched into these stones are ancient symbols, looking almost like swirling mazes. As you travel deeper, the world gets darker and darker, until everything becomes pitch black. In fact, you cannot see your party members beside you. All you can make out is your own body, and the darkness surrounding you...
> - [[The Ferryman]] ushers the characters off, wishing them luck before sailing away.
> - The cave and hillock are actually a burial mound for a long lost ancient people.
> - The deepest portions of the cave becoming an opening to an "ethereal void", where visions of nightmarish creatures and scenarios play out before the characters, attempting to render them mad.
> - While they are in this void, the characters will not be able to find an exit, nor each other, until they encounter the nightmare within.
> - Have them each describe their characters worst fears.
> - While the party traverses the void, have them make a Wisdom saving through. Consult the madness table below, this madness lasts for 1d10 hours.

> [!recite|no-i nbrd] Thatticus' Castle
>> [!boxed|no-t]
>> 	The dense treeline opens up before you into a marshy wetland. In the distance, the ancient ruins of a castle stand upon a small hill, with crumbling walls wrapping around a large central tower. From the tower emanates the swirling Vortex of Souls. Rising into it are the twisted spectral figures of spirits in anguish, clearly visible even from this distance. Traversing the marsh isn't difficult, but it is slow going. Inside the compound, an old wooden door stands open at the base of the tower, leading further in.
> 
> On entering the base:
>> [!boxed|no-t]
>> 	You are greeted with what appears to be a sort of laboratory. Along the sides of the room are desks covered with alchemical equipment, as well as the bones of various creatures - predominantly humanoid ones. An operating table with a humanoid corpse is pushed to the back of the room, though each of its limbs looks like they came from a different creature and were stitched together. In the middle of the room, and seemingly at the epicenter of the vortex, is a machine of sorts, with a metallic structure holding a black crystal skill at its center. Bolts of black and green electricity arc from the skill and into the metal casing, which redirects it upwards and into the sky above. Surrounding this machine is a bubble of shifting and swirling figures - more anguished spirits, forced and layered atop each other, a few hands and faces sticking out of this awful shield. And, before all of this, is a figure. He turns around to face you as you enter the room, a crooked, menacing smile on his pale face. His clothing must have, at one point, been well made, something a nobleman would have worn, but it has now become soiled and tattered and, in some places, blood-stained. Over the top of his clothing, this man has constructed a sort of armour with what appear to be bones. In his hand he clutches close to him a book, kept in a relatively pristine condition compared to your surroundings. His hair is long and messy, brown streaked with white. However, his most outstanding feature is his face. Most of it appears normal, if a little gaunt; however, a large portion of his left side is rotted and decayed, as if he is himself partly a corpse. He looks upon you with menacing amusement before opening up his arms and proclaiming, "Welcome, foolish mortals! Prepare to die!"
> - [[Thatticus Shorn]] attacks any intruders into his base. He is beyond reasoning, deeply entrenched in madness, and sees himself as the only rightful ruler of the dead. And he wants *everyone* under his domain.
>
>> [!recite|no-i clean] The Tome of Spirits
>> - On taking damage [[Thatticus Shorn]] rolls a constitution saving throw DC 5 + the amount of damage taken to keep hold of the [[Tome of Spirits]].
>> - The [[Tome of Spirits]] is magically reinforced, has an AC of 10, 12 hp, has +4 to con saving throws and automatically fails all other saving throws.
>> - After dropping the Tome, Thatticus' control loosens slightly and two **Wandering Spirits** from the shield start to lash out at the book to end the spell.
>> - Picking up the book increases it's AC to match that of it's holder. Thatticus will focus his attacks on anyone holding the book. 
>> - When picking up the Tome and then at the at the start of each turn when holding it, make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw as the book attempts to whisper into your mind. If you fail then until next turn any attacks made against Thatticus are made with disadvantage and any attacks made against you by Thatticus are made with advantage.
>
>> [!recite|no-i clean] The Effigy
>> - To stop the vortex, the party must destroy the effigy.
>> - In the center of the room is the effigy, protected by a shield of tormented souls that surrounds it.
>> - If a character attempts to pass through the shield, or attack it with a melee weapon, they are unable to, and take 15 necrotic damage. All ranged attacks that target the effigy are knocked out of the air.
>> - If [[Thatticus Shorn]] is defeated, the souls are released and the shield no longer functions. After which, any damage will destroy the effigy.

>> [!boxed|no-t]
>> As the effigy is destroyed, there is a whirling howl before... silence. The ethereal winds above you come to a halt as the vortex is dispelled. The once warped and shifting landscape settles down and returns to its natural state. It is over, and the spirits that were trapped and lost are now free.
