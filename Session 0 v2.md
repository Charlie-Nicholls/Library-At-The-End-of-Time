When we started I was super new to DMing and didn't really know what DMing style I was gonna like. It turns out I've forgotten some of the things I originally pitched/communicated badly but also there has been some conscious deviation from that pitch when I learned we were only going to have 4 players, who seemed to be able to schedule consistently enough to have everyone there, and in discovering what I enjoyed running. My ideal vision is getting a cast for medium length linear stories and then moving on to something completely different, (could be with new people) in the style of Dimension 20. I'm not interested in large multi-year 50+ session campaigns. I am however willing to try going back more towards the original pitch, so I've laid out a few ways we could go, feel free to suggest something yourself too.

Options for how to continue campaign:
- **1. Continue with current plan**
	- Give choices of missions that relate to defeating Scheherazade, functionally one-shots to collect objects or info
	- Optionally continue on to the final arc I had in mind
	- These are both of flexible length
- **2. Shortened Campaign**
	- Continue in the same style with a similar plan but significantly shorten the intended time frame, cut out the second arc
	- Would have a more continuous story for now but would then leave us able to do either
	- **2.1 Shortened -> Original pure one shots**
		- Can keep same characters (assuming they live), or change or have several at different levels
		- Then would go back to having no continuous plot threads more in the style originally intended, see 3.
	- **2.2 Shortened -> Different campaign**
		- Start a new linear story in a similar style to recent sessions with new characters/setting
		- Would not be long stories, probably ~8-12 sessions over 6-10 months, less freedom than 2.1 but more payoff
- **3. Revert to Original pitch**
	- This is the one I'm least sure what it looks like so would definitely require further discussion but likely:
	- Finish Scheherazade story as a one shot
	- I would probably cut 99% of The Library stuff here and we'd just start each time at the place with the mission brief in hand
	- Less sure of what these might look like so I'd definitely want some suggestions
		- Can't see how I would do these in literally 1 session as they're too short unless this is something people really want, but they would all stand alone.
		- Level changing. Cameron reminded me of this and I forgot I suggested it originally. This would become possible, though I would request that we make character sheets ahead of time for each level we might want to do as we cannot do it at the start of sessions.
		- DM changing. Not sure how much people want to DM their own one shots, but it was part of the original pitch and if we did this style I'd like to encourage it more.
		- Combat/RP ratios. I'm not interested in doing majority combat sessions, which one shotting tends to lean towards. I can adjust the balance a little, but if what you want is to just beat up monsters with friends I'm not the DM for that. Even with trying to make combat more interesting, which I have been trying to do, I wouldn't enjoy running it if there wasn't some story/mystery/role play in most sessions even if it has no wider implications beyond that adventure.
- **4. Big changes**
	- May involve swapping players if we're not looking for the same thing
	- Pausing current campaign and picking up later
	- Someone else becoming primary DM
	- Find some other excuse to hang out instead. The point of this is to hang out and have fun, I love dnd and have been using it as a way to keep hanging out with my friends, but ultimately the only reason we're doing that with dnd is because I was the one who tried to organise stuff and that's my preference. Maybe something else might be better and I can find other people to play dnd with.

Without feedback steering me otherwise, the way the one shots would look would be similar to the 1001 Arabian nights adventure in structure, with that sort of arriving in a place, there's probably some mystery, some role play to figure stuff out, maybe a minor fight, some more working out what's going on leading up to a big final confrontation. Something that has a concept i find interesting more than, I pick a monster out of the MM and stick it in a place. And they would take a few sessions to finish, but then there'd be no connective tissue and you can forget everything after you leave.
