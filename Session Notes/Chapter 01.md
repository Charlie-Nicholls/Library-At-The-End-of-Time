### Summary
- Summarise player deaths.
- Seeing a book be stolen by a cloaked figure, following them, overhearing someone talking to a Thri-keen, [[Clickity Clackity]] recognised the first voice.
- You were told about the book [[Practical Magichemsitry]] being overdue from [[Huck]] and that you needed to visit [[Flintfall]] in the [[The Badlands]] to retrieve it.
- [[Abblesmith's Guide to the Badlands Desert]], which was supposed to open the [[The Book Wormhole]] was missing and [[Chan-tho]] instead found a biography of some outlaw called [[Wild Willie]] who grew up in nearby [[Eastwood]] which dropped you about an hours walk from [[Flintfall]].
- [[Professor Werblzwerth]] told you a portal would be opened where you were dropped off, 5 hours after you arrive, an hour after that and if you still haven't gotten back a day after. If you miss all of them you would have to contact them through whatever a local sect of [[The Library]] you can find
- On arriving you found [[Huck]] was using the book to repair himself but [[Eddie Van Patten]]'s mercenaries had come in and stolen it and were readying a train to [[Eastwood]]
- After some mixed success with distracting the guards, [[Nivea]] and [[Clickity Clackity]] were caught sabotaging the tracks and a fight broke out.
- You decimated them in what could've been a dangerous fight, leaving two of the four non-lethally having their skulls disintegrated.
- After a quick fight with the conductor, you retrieve the key to the vault.
- In the train you found a plethora of weapons, a note detailing the layout of a saloon called [[The Last Chance]] with crosses marked on it, [[Practical Magichemsitry]] with a few pages torn out, a huge amount of gold, and [[Clickity Clackity]] found a note which read "A book detailing the production of the necessary explosives is currently possessed by [[Huck]], the Sheriff of [[Flintfall]]. Then proceed to the Last Chance - [[The Editors]]".
- You learned that the missing pages were sent ahead by another mercenary on horseback.
- You are left with the choice to either return to [[The Library]] with most of the book and potentially leaving [[The Badlands]] to the fate of whatever [[Eddie Van Patten]] is planning to do with the pages if [[The Library]] doesn't intervene or following the pages to [[Eastwood]].
