### Summary
Dendrin was taken by Tibword down a nonsense of corridors to meet with his patron: The Trickster, who he learned is an Archetype looking to find people with strong moral fibre. They revealed that Professor Werblzwerth originally rejected Dendrin as a candidate Librarian and it was only on their insistence that he was included. Wanting to test him, they told Tibword to give the party the Tome of Spirits case, promising more power through the warlock pact if they approved of Dendrin's actions.

The Tome of Spirits was a necromantic book that vanished from the library many chapters ago and found in the hands of the Holy Witches coven. It was thought lost but recent reports of evil in the area the witches operate indicated it had resurfaced. Professor Werblzwerth gave Dreisfor a new version of his adaptable gun before sending you off to the swamps of Mirewood.
After obtaining a small steamboat from a guard who informed you that the few reports they'd had from Mirewood were of madness and curses, you travelled down a winding river where you arrived at the town to find it abandoned and decrepit. A spirit attacked you on arrival after which you explore and met the Lothbrook family who told you the last thing they remembered was a green light emanating from the old church and a creature had recently moved into the tower.

Exploring the tower lead you to finding the hideout of who you would later learn was Marie Anchester and a Cloak of Billowing which Nivea took. Scouting out the church you saw the hostile spirit of the Bishop guarding the inside so you decided to first explore the graveyard and connected catacombs. Underneath you met the undead Marie Anchester who was guarding an idol. On defeating her and breaking the idol the curse on the town was lifted. Going back to explore the church tower you found a clearly cursed Dagger holding in place the scroll that was controlling Marie. 
Marie had a note on her with instructions to place another idol on The Belle of Orlea, which the spirits of the Lothbrook's told you is the boat their daughter had left on. Wanting to help, they offered to enter your bodies to give you additional abilities, which Nivea and Clickity took them up on. 

When you found it, it became clear the boat had crashed, and exploring it you found no-one living, only signs of a brutal murder spree. Exploring the ship, you ascended the decks until Dendrin wandered off and was ambushed by the horrifying creature Aemily Lothbrook had become. Killing the creature proved only a temporary solution as it came back to attack you again before you found the key and entered the storage deck, where the idol was held. A final fight with the monster was ended when you rushed ahead to break the idol it was defending. Before passing on, the spirits of the Lothbrooks passed on their gifts to you, which would be available as long as you were near Mirewood. But before they could completely pass on, a massive storm broke out. A vortex of souls drawing in all the spirits in the area.
