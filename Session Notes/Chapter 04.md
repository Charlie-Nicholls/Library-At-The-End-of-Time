### Summary
A vortex of souls broke out over the swamps of Mirewood, causing a cyprus tree to fall and severely damage your steamboat. Fortunately, a friendly spirit calling themselves The Ferryman arrived and offered to sail you through the swamps closer to the centre of the vortex. There were two paths towards the epicentre, directly through the deep swamps or diverting through the cave of sorrows. After taking you as far as they could, they dropped you off to make your way through the gnarled tree's of the deep swamp. Whilst Nivea nimbly swung through the trees avoiding their reaching branches, Dendrin stomped through the centre of the swamp out of their reach. Clickity and Dreisfor were less successful, attempts to avoid the deeper areas of the swamp lead them to wander closer to the branches of the trees which swiped out and cut them.

Arriving at the centre of the vortex, you found the tower of the necromancer Thatticus Shorn. Nivea successfully snuck up to the door and peered inside, seeing Thatticus tending to the vortex's origins - another skull idol surrounded by a shield of trapped souls. Meanwhile, Dreisfor jumped in an attempted to look in through the slits in the wall, landing loudly and alerting him to your presence. When the fight started, Thatticus was clutching the Tome of Souls tight, and over the course of the fight it was dropped and picked up several times by him, Nivea and Clickity, with the book attempting to control whoever held it. Without the book his control over the soul shield was slightly loosened and a wandering spirit was freed and began to attack the book, which was had a spell embedded in it to protect it. Dreisfor had been struggling, rolling nat 2s 5 times, and trying to hide behind a door, opening it in the process. But he came in clutch, scoring two massive hits to kill Thatticus at the end, just as the magical shield for the book failed. Clickity quickly picked it up to shield it from the attacking spirits, as Dreisfor shot and destroyed the idol, ending the vortex and lifting the curse that had held Mirewood. Many hours of trudging through the swamp later, you re-met the guard, informed him that the boat he lent you was destroyed, the curse was lifted, lied badly to hide you still had the cursed book, turned into a crow and ran away into the forest to travel back to the library where you were met by Tibword, Aldra and a grey haired man you don't recognise.
