### Summary
On returning to [[The Library]] and debriefing with [[Aldra Lamnem|Aldra]], she took [[Nivea]], [[Clickity Clackity]] and [[Dreisfor]] to the section of [[The Library]] containing books on [[The Badlands]]. You found it in turmoil, with books being unwritten, rewritten and appearing before you. You rushed to investigate but the chaos made it difficult, only being able to grab a book about the life of [[John Wilde]] by [[Cornelia Kid]] which detailed the work he did after you saved him in fighting the mob. Though the fact this book didn't exist before suggests this is a change to the timeline. Finally, [[Aldra Lamnem|Aldra]] took [[Nivea]] to one side to tell her **[[The Trickster]] had lost his [[Books of Self|Book of Self]] and would be recruiting them to help get it back. But [[The Authors]] had grown suspicious of him. So, while they aren't really supposed to read [[Books of Self]], if she could secretly take a peek and see what he had been up to it would be appreciated.** Meanwhile, [[Dendrin]] was speaking to [[The Trickster]] who was thrilled with how the mission went. He asked to see the book he gave [[Dendrin]] and found, much to his delight, that it was being unwritten. He explained that it would go to [[The Unobserved]], the realm of alternate history, full of the things and people that could have been but never were. 

Not long after, [[The Trickster]] gathered you all and introduced himself as [[Dendrin]]'s patron. They explained that there exists [[Books of Self]], books that are written by [[Actus]] which document the lives of every being in the universe. They are usually hidden deep inside [[The Library]] away from anyone trying to read that, and that usually the retrieval of such a book would be a quest of itself, but that each book takes on some of the properties of its subject. Like them, their book has a tendency towards illusion and teleportation magics and dislikes authority or confinement so has a tendency to go missing. They tracked it to the [[Wandering Museum]], but the people who run it deny they have it and he cannot enter himself due to a *magic circle* enchantment. As such, they needed you to steal it. Providing you with two [[Hearring]]s that you can use to communicate with each other and [[The Trickster]], he *plane shifted* you to the museum.

Given you had 3 days before the museum moved on, the first was spent doing reconnaissance. Security would ask you to leave any weapons at the door, though [[Clickity Clackity|Clickity]] managed to semi-successfully sneak a scythe through in his trousers. [[Dendrin]] was quickly able to identify that each exhibit was surrounded by some sort of protective force magic that would need to be disabled. [[Dreisfor]] found the museum was littered with [[Sphinx Statue]]s that each had 5 gems embedded in the top, one of which was lit up. [[Nivea]] examined them and believed they are a magical part of the security. [[Clickity Clackity|Clickity]] looked at the arcane calligraphy exhibit that detailed how to disable a rune while [[Dreisfor]] scouted some of the staff only doors, finding one which was out of line of sight of the guards and seemed simple to open.

Moving to the second floor, [[Dendrin]] found that near the mythril exhibit was a three part rune that glowed a faint red. Asking [[The Trickster]], they said they weren't good at runes but speculated that it was an important part of security. [[Dreisfor]] overheard an argument between a guard and [[Rinaldo Bardi]] before he was escorted out, discovering he was a posh kid who used to have a job here through his uncle but was recently fired. [[Clickity Clackity|Clickity]] investigated the toilets extremely thoroughly and was confused by the plumbing. [[Dendrin]] went to the café and spoke to a woman who'd worked there for years - 2 years in fact - learning that the Dominic Bardi exhibit was new, as was much of the Egyptology section, and that the next day the café would host a Sketch and Sip that would serve free drinks. Which seems redundant given he ran away without paying for his coffee. [[Nivea]] took a look at the Egyptology section, finding various statues of pyramids, sphinxes, a pharaoh and the god Set, though walking past the statue she got the distinct feeling something was wrong.
