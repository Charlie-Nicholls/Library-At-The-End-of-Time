### Summary
[[Nivea]] found a suspicious book in the Egyptology exhibit, clutched in the hand of a statue of Ra. Closer examination revealed it had hieroglyphs on the cover, is shrouded in illusion magic, with a another thread of magic connecting it to the pharaoh exhibit. After the [[Wandering Museum]] closed for the day, [[Dreisfor]] spoke to [[Rinaldo Bardi]] outside, learning about his history with the museum, how his uncle [[The Noble|Dominic]] got him the job and was angry at him for losing it, threatening to cut him off if he doesn't get it back. [[Dreisfor]] convinced him to go in the next day and make a scene.

On the second day, semi-successful attempts to sneak in taught you that the [[Wizard Guard]] at the entrance has a *Gem of Seeing*, fortunately, they didn't think to look up and see [[Clickity Clackity]] wild-shaped into a spider on the ceiling. He then proceeded directly the toilets, down the sink and through the plumbing, narrowly avoiding being forcefully shifted back to normal by a torrent of water. [[Dendrin]] did a full scan of the museum looking for more runes, and found one on the ceiling in the café. [[Nivea]] spoke to [[Dr Carda Halftusk]] about translating the hieroglyphs and she took her to the library in the staff area, where [[Luca Lomeo]], an overworked grad student was working by a stack of books. While [[Dr Carda Halftusk|Carda]] was translating, [[Nivea]] examined the books and found one detailed how the magic behind the security system works. [[Dr Carda Halftusk|Carda]] eventually said the hieroglyphs translated roughly to "The Set" and [[Nivea]] has so far decided to keep the existence of the book and her knowledge of the security to herself. 

Meanwhile, [[Clickity Clackity|Clickity]] had found himself inside a large septic tank and, deciding he couldn't escape as a spider, shifted back into a 7ft. tall thri'keen and cast shatter on the tank. The resulting pressure wave exploded the septic tank, flooding the room with sewage. He re-spidered himself as a guard ran in to check on the sound but wasn't able to fully allay suspicions. [[Dreisfor]] watched as sewage began to leak into the glyph exhibit and the guards quickly reorganised to keep people away from the area. This thinned the guards near the first rune, which [[Dendrin]] used, along with an unseen servant, to disable it. It was at this moment [[Rinaldo Bardi]] entered and began berating the guards about the situation. [[Dreisfor]] had worked out that the gems in the head of the [[Sphinx Statue]]s functioned as an alert level and, with four of five lit up, took this opportunity [[Rinaldo Bardi|Rinaldo]] provided to sneak into the back room where [[Clickity Clackity|Clickity]] is.
