### Notes
- nat 20 stealth from nivea to scratch out the rune prestidigitation on pipes from dendrin
- clickity & dreis for eventually get through the arcane lock on the archives, finding it shrouded in magical darkness
- Clickity explores the room as a spider while nivea meditates suspiciously while shaking the floor
- Find the final rune, Dreisfor jumps up and scratches it out
- Nivea casts darkness to allow Dreisfor to escape, but the guards are clearly alert to someone trying to rob the museum
- Overnight Dreisfor is visited by The Noble, as Dominic Bardi, and makes a deal with him, a magic item in return for disabling the magic circle and not telling the others about the deal. He also provides him with a map of the museum with the security marked
- Dendrin misty steps through easy to pick door, clickity spiders through upstairs door, dreisfor picks lock.
- Dendrin wants to look at the archive to find the book
- Dreisfor + clickity explore upstairs, finding keys to the staff areas + archive in dr halftusks desk, avoiding Luca in the process
- Calling dendrin upstairs, they then lift the quartz table in the scriptorium and disable the magic circle rune
- Meanwhile nivea has cast darkness, punched out the glass on several exhibits and stolen the book
- The guards immediately start patrolling and the sphinxes come to life
- Nivea reads a page of the book
- The Trickster enters the museum when the magic circle drops, The Noble follows shortly after
- The Noble heads directly upstairs towards where he knows the book is and The Trickster sees him and panics, calling the others out to stop him getting the book
- Tells them he is an Archetype called The Noble who was expelled from The Library long ago and hates him
- A chase occurs, with Nivea using monk dexterity and control of darkness to evade The Noble
- The Noble attempts to use enchantment magic and his influence over the guards and sphinxes to slow them down
- Dreisfor, disguised, blocks him on the stairs
- Clickity freezes the stairs and he slips down
- Dendrin takes the book and misty steps closer to the exit, evading the lunging guards
- Nivea, with the help of The Trickster, charms the guards with her good looks after being tackled
- Clickity explodes the replacement septic tank, seemingly for no reason but spite
- What seemed like a sure escape suddenly becomes tense as guards near the entrance grapple Dendrin and sphinxes crowd the room, but they just about escape and The Trickster plane shifts them back home
- The Trickster takes back his book
- Dreisfor receives a package with his magic item with a label saying "Unlike some, I honour my deals. - The Noble"
